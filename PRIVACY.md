# SPLINED Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026  
**Publisher:** Psycotix  
**Application:** S:P:L:I:N:E:D (SPLINED)

## Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the SPLINED desktop application published by Psycotix.

SPLINED is a local desktop application for discovering, reviewing, editing, and managing album artwork and related music metadata in user-selected music libraries.

## Information SPLINED accesses

SPLINED may access information that the user explicitly configures or selects, including:

- music files and folders;
- artist, album, release, recording, and other embedded music metadata;
- album artwork and embedded artwork;
- application configuration and interface settings;
- the SPLINED SQLite media database;
- provider credential and authorization files;
- application logs, history, caches, and diagnostic information;
- local, mapped-drive, removable-storage, and UNC/network locations selected by the user.

This information is processed locally on the user's device or on storage locations selected by the user.

## Network requests and third-party services

When enabled by the user, SPLINED may send music-related search and lookup information to third-party metadata and artwork services in order to provide application functionality.

This information may include artist names, album names, release titles, recording identifiers, MusicBrainz identifiers, artwork search terms, and other metadata necessary to perform a lookup.

Supported or configurable services may include Apple/iTunes, Deezer, MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Fanart.tv, Last.fm, Discogs, Amazon, and other providers supported by the installed SPLINED version.

Network services necessarily receive technical connection information such as the user's IP address. Third-party providers process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## Credentials and authorization tokens

Provider credentials, API keys, cookies, and authorization tokens configured by the user are stored in files or locations selected by the user.

SPLINED does not operate a central Psycotix service that collects or stores users' provider credentials, music libraries, SPLINED SQLite databases, or application configuration.

Credentials may be transmitted directly to the applicable third-party provider when required for authentication or an authorized request.

## Diagnostics and logs

SPLINED may create local diagnostic logs and run reports containing application activity, file paths, provider responses, metadata, and error information.

These files remain under the user's control and are not automatically transmitted to Psycotix. A user may choose to provide diagnostic information when requesting support.

Users should not include credential contents, access tokens, passwords, or other secrets when sharing diagnostics.

## Telemetry, advertising, and sale of data

The current SPLINED desktop application does not include Psycotix-operated advertising or developer-operated usage telemetry.

Psycotix does not sell or rent personal information processed by SPLINED.

Microsoft Store, operating-system services, network providers, and third-party services may independently collect information under their own privacy policies.

## Data retention and user control

SPLINED stores application data only in locations controlled or configured by the user, including the application's data directory and any configured database, credential, cache, history, or log locations.

Users can remove SPLINED-owned configuration, interface settings, credentials, caches, logs, history, and database files from their systems. Users are responsible for backups of files and libraries they choose to modify with SPLINED.

Uninstalling an installed package may not remove external files or locations that the user configured outside the application's package-managed storage.

## Children

SPLINED is a general-purpose music-library utility and is not directed specifically to children. Psycotix does not knowingly use SPLINED to collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SPLINED's data practices or supported services change. The current version will be published in this repository with its effective date.
